# Trading Examples - Brooks H2/L2 Pullback Indicator

This document provides practical trading examples using the Brooks H2/L2 Pullback Indicator.

## Example 1: H2 Bull Flag in ES 5-Minute Chart

### Market Context
- **Instrument**: E-mini S&P 500 (ES)
- **Timeframe**: 5-minute chart
- **Market Phase**: Bull trend, morning session
- **EMA**: Price consistently above rising 20 EMA

### Pattern Development

**Stage 1 - Initial Uptrend**
```
9:30 AM - 10:00 AM: Strong buying, price moves from 4500 to 4515
- Multiple strong bullish bars
- Price well above 20 EMA
- EMA is rising
```

**Stage 2 - First Pullback Leg**
```
10:00 AM - 10:15 AM: First pullback leg down
- Swing high forms at 4515
- Price pulls back to 4510 (near EMA at 4509)
- First swing low forms at 4509
```

**Stage 3 - Second Pullback Leg**
```
10:15 AM - 10:25 AM: Second pullback leg down
- Small rally to 4512 (lower than first swing high)
- Second decline to 4508 (near EMA)
- Second swing low forms at 4507
- Two-legged pullback complete
```

**Stage 4 - H2 Signal**
```
10:25 AM: Strong bullish bar appears
- Open: 4507.50
- Close: 4509.25 (near high)
- Strong buying pressure visible
- GREEN ARROW + "H2" appears below the bar
```

### Trade Execution

**Entry**
- Buy stop at 4509.50 (1 tick above signal bar high)
- Order triggers at 10:30 AM

**Stop Loss**
- Below second swing low: 4506.50
- Risk: 3 points (12 ticks)

**Target**
- Measured move: 15 points (same as initial leg)
- First target: 4515 (prior swing high)
- Final target: 4525 (measured move)

**Result**
- Target 1 hit at 10:50 AM (+5.5 points)
- Partial exit, move stop to breakeven
- Target 2 hit at 11:20 AM (+15.5 points)
- Risk/Reward: 1:5.1

## Example 3: Failed H2 Setup (What NOT to Trade)

### Market Context
- **Instrument**: Crude Oil (CL)
- **Timeframe**: 5-minute chart
- **Issue**: Choppy market, not in clear trend

### Why This Failed

**Problems Identified:**
1. **Weak Trend**: Price oscillating around EMA
2. **Poor Signal Bar**: Small doji-like bar, not strong bullish
3. **Context**: Multiple failed breakouts recently
4. **Range-Bound**: Trading in 2-point range for past hour

**What Indicator Showed:**
- H2 arrow appeared (met technical criteria)
- But context was poor

**What Happened:**
- Entry triggered
- Price immediately reversed
- Stop hit for full loss

**Lesson:**
Not every H2/L2 signal is tradeable. Context matters:
- Need clear trend
- Need strong signal bars
- Avoid choppy, range-bound markets

## Example 4: Multiple H2 Signals in Same Trend

### Market Context
- **Instrument**: EUR/USD Forex
- **Timeframe**: 15-minute chart
- **Scenario**: Strong bull trend with multiple pullbacks

### First H2 Signal
```
Morning: First H2 at 1.0850
- Entry: 1.0851
- Target 1: 1.0865 ✓ Hit
- Target 2: 1.0880 ✓ Hit
```

### Second H2 Signal
```
Midday: Another H2 at 1.0875 (after consolidation)
- Entry: 1.0876
- Target 1: 1.0890 ✓ Hit
- Target 2: 1.0905 ✓ Hit
```

### Third H2 Signal
```
Afternoon: Late H2 at 1.0900
- Entry: 1.0901
- Result: Failed, trend exhausted
- Hit stop at 1.0895
```

### Analysis
- **First two H2s**: Early in strong trend, excellent setups
- **Third H2**: Late in trend, lower probability
- **Key Learning**: Be selective later in trends
  - Look for signs of exhaustion (smaller legs, weaker bars)
  - Consider reducing position size or skipping late signals

## Example 5: H2 with Supporting Confluence

### Market Context
- **Instrument**: Gold (GC)
- **Timeframe**: 60-minute chart
- **Multiple Confirmations**: Best setup type

### Pattern Development
Standard H2 forms at $2,050

### Additional Confluence Factors

1. **Higher Timeframe Trend**
   - Daily chart in strong uptrend
   - Aligns with H2 signal

2. **Support Level**
   - H2 signal bar at key support zone ($2,048-$2,050)
   - Previous support held multiple times

3. **Volume**
   - High volume on signal bar
   - Shows strong buyer interest

4. **Fibonacci**
   - Pullback to 61.8% retracement
   - Common reversal zone

5. **Round Number**
   - $2,050 psychological level
   - Attracts buyers

### Trade Result
- Higher probability setup
- Multiple confirmations
- Hit all targets
- Risk/Reward: 1:4

### Lesson
Best H2/L2 setups have multiple confirming factors:
- Indicator signal (H2/L2)
- Support/Resistance
- Higher timeframe alignment
- Volume confirmation
- Technical levels (Fibonacci, pivots, etc.)

## Risk Management Examples

### Position Sizing Calculator

**Account Size**: $50,000
**Risk Per Trade**: 1% = $500

#### Example 1: ES H2 Trade
```
Risk: 3 points = 12 ticks
Value per tick: $12.50
Total risk per contract: 12 × $12.50 = $150

Position size: $500 ÷ $150 = 3.33 contracts
Trade: 3 contracts (round down for safety)
```

#### Example 2: NQ L2 Trade
```
Risk: 6.5 points = 26 ticks
Value per tick: $5
Total risk per contract: 26 × $5 = $130

Position size: $500 ÷ $130 = 3.84 contracts
Trade: 3 contracts
```

### Stop Loss Guidelines

**Too Tight** (Avoid)
```
Stop just below signal bar
Problem: Normal volatility stops you out
Result: Many stopped out trades
```

**Good Practice**
```
Stop below most recent swing low (H2)
Stop above most recent swing high (L2)
Gives room for normal price action
```

**Too Wide** (Avoid)
```
Stop far beyond swings "just in case"
Problem: Risk too large
Result: Poor risk/reward, big losses
```

## Common Scenarios & Solutions

### Scenario 1: Signal appears but you're unsure
**Solution**: 
- Check higher timeframe trend
- Verify signal bar quality
- Assess recent price action
- When in doubt, skip it

### Scenario 2: Multiple signals in quick succession
**Solution**:
- Take first quality signal
- Let it play out before taking another
- Don't overtrade same trend

### Scenario 3: Signal appears at end of day
**Solution**:
- Consider overnight risk
- May skip or reduce size
- Or take with wider stops

### Scenario 4: News event approaching
**Solution**:
- Skip signals right before major news
- Wait for post-news trends to establish
- Avoid low-probability environments

### Scenario 5: Strong trend but no H2/L2 signals
**Solution**:
- Indicator doesn't catch every opportunity
- Use your own analysis too
- May take H1 or H3 with other confirmation
