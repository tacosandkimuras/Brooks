# Quick Reference Guide - Brooks H2/L2 Pullback Indicator

## One-Page Cheat Sheet

### Visual Signals
```
🟢 ↑ H2  = Long entry signal (bull trend)
🔴 ↓ L2  = Short entry signal (bear trend)
🔵 •    = Swing high marker
🟣 •    = Swing low marker
━━━━    = 20 EMA (yellow line)
```

### Entry Rules

#### H2 Bull Flag (Long)
- ✅ Price above 20 EMA
- ✅ EMA is rising
- ✅ Two pullback legs down identified
- ✅ Strong bullish signal bar appears
- 📍 **Entry**: Buy stop above signal bar high
- 🛑 **Stop**: Below recent swing low

#### L2 Bear Flag (Short)
- ✅ Price below 20 EMA
- ✅ EMA is falling
- ✅ Two pullback legs up identified
- ✅ Strong bearish signal bar appears
- 📍 **Entry**: Sell stop below signal bar low
- 🛑 **Stop**: Above recent swing high

### Quick Checklist Before Every Trade

**Trend Check**
- [ ] Price clearly above/below EMA?
- [ ] EMA rising (bull) or falling (bear)?
- [ ] Multiple bars in trend direction?

**Pattern Check**
- [ ] Two distinct pullback legs visible?
- [ ] Pullback approached EMA?
- [ ] Signal bar is strong (not doji/weak)?

**Context Check**
- [ ] Market trending (not choppy)?
- [ ] No major resistance/support in way?
- [ ] Not too late in the trend?

**Risk Check**
- [ ] Stop distance reasonable?
- [ ] Position sized correctly?
- [ ] Risk/Reward ratio at least 1:1.5?

### Target Options

**Conservative**
- Exit at prior swing high/low
- R/R typically 1:1 to 1:2

**Standard**
- Partial at swing point
- Runner to measured move
- R/R typically 1:2 to 1:3

**Aggressive**
- Hold for measured move or more
- Trail stop with swing points
- R/R potentially 1:3+

### When to SKIP the Trade

❌ **Market Conditions**
- Choppy/sideways price action
- Trading range (not trending)
- Low volume/holiday session
- Major news event imminent

❌ **Pattern Issues**
- Weak/small signal bar
- Unclear pullback structure
- Only one pullback leg
- Signal very late in trend

❌ **Technical Issues**
- Signal at major resistance (H2)
- Signal at major support (L2)
- Risk too large vs account
- Trend conflict with higher timeframe

### Position Sizing Formula

```
Maximum Risk = Account Size × Risk %
              (e.g., $50,000 × 1% = $500)

Position Size = Maximum Risk ÷ Stop Distance in $
                (e.g., $500 ÷ $150 = 3 contracts)
```

### Common Mistakes

1. **Taking H1 instead of H2**
   - Wait for second leg!

2. **Ignoring EMA trend filter**
   - Don't take H2 below EMA
   - Don't take L2 above EMA

3. **Using stops too tight**
   - Give room for normal movement

4. **Overtrading same trend**
   - Be selective with late signals

5. **Ignoring signal bar quality**
   - Need strong directional bars

### Troubleshooting

**No signals appearing?**
- Check if in trending market
- Try lower swing strength
- Verify enough historical bars loaded

**Too many signals?**
- Increase swing strength
- Be more selective with trades
- Focus on strongest setups only

**Signals too late?**
- Normal - swing confirmation requires bars
- Use to confirm your own analysis
- Signals are not predictive

**Wrong trend direction?**
- Check EMA is visible
- Verify EMA rising/falling
- May need stronger trend

### Risk Management Rules

**Position Sizing**
- Never risk more than 1-2% per trade
- Scale in/out when appropriate
- Adjust size based on stop distance

**Stop Loss**
- Always use stops
- Place at logical swing points
- Don't move stop against you

**Take Profit**
- Define targets before entry
- Consider partial exits
- Trail stops in strong trends

**Daily Loss Limit**
- Set max daily loss (e.g., 3-5%)
- Stop trading when hit
- Come back tomorrow fresh
